# KaamSetu explainer storyboard

**Working title:** KaamSetu — Connecting Skills. Creating Opportunities.  
**Target runtime:** 35 seconds  
**Frame:** 16:9, 1920 × 1080, 24 fps  
**Delivery:** muted H.264 MP4 at `static/videos/kaamsetu-explainer.mp4`  
**Visual language:** natural Indian workplace photography, restrained camera movement, deep navy shadows, indigo/blue interface accents, white typography, clean match cuts. Keep important action in the central 70% for mobile crops.

## Edit and sound direction

- Use six deliberate beats with gentle dissolves or phone-screen match cuts; no unrelated montage inserts.
- Let workplace ambience lead, with a restrained, hopeful instrumental bed. The homepage master remains muted.
- Use large, high-contrast sans-serif captions and keep each card to one readable idea.
- Loop only by fading the final navy end card into the opening dark workplace frame.
