Voice typing

When you tap a mic button, your browser handles speech recognition and may send audio to its recognition service. KaamSetu receives only the text you submit in a form; microphone audio is not uploaded by voice typing.

AI help chat

When the Gemini assistant is enabled, it sends your question, recent chat context, account role, and language to Google. KaamSetu does not save the chat. Google may use free-tier prompts to improve its products, so do not include passwords, OTPs, phone numbers, or private details.
